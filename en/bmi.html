How Body Mass Index (BMI) and BMR Are Calculated

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a globally recognized screening metric that relates your body weight to your height. Coupled with your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)—the number of calories your body burns at complete rest—it provides an essential baseline for nutrition, fitness, and weight management.

Standard Metric BMI Formula:
BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ [ Height (m) × Height (m) ]
Mifflin-St Jeor BMR Equation:
Men: BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) − 161

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) measures calories burned at complete rest, whereas TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) multiplies your BMR by an activity factor (1.2 for sedentary up to 1.9 for athletes) to estimate total daily calorie burn.
