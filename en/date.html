How Date Duration and Calendar Arithmetic Work

Calculating exact durations across calendar months and years requires handling varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and Gregorian leap-year rules without daylight saving time (DST) drift. OmniCalc normalizes dates to UTC midnight timestamps to guarantee exact integer day counts.

Gregorian Leap Year Rule:
A year is a leap year (366 days) if divisible by 4, except century years unless divisible by 400.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Local time zones that observe Daylight Saving Time have one 23-hour day and one 25-hour day each year. By converting calendar dates to UTC midnight before dividing by 86,400,000 milliseconds per day, OmniCalc eliminates any DST rounding errors.
